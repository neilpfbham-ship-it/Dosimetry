Tolerance & reaction doses

A acute: during or within a few weeks S sub-acute: 2–6 months after L late: months to years after
dot colour follows dose, low to high · highlighted = highlighted on the sheet
All doses assume 2Gy a day, 5 days a week, with moderate target volumes. So the number of fractions (#) is roughly half the dose in Gy. The tolerance dose is the minimum dose that will result in a significant complication within normal tissue.
Transcribed from the BCU tolerance dose table (pp. 105–106). The printed sheet is the only version accepted in clinical assessments, so check anything that looks off against it.
